    You’ll see the screen change from Next Up to Approaching to Now Playing. Pause or replay using the tour controls when you need to.

How do I listen through my vehicle’s speakers?

While parked, pair your phone using your vehicle’s Bluetooth instructions, then select Bluetooth audio on the stereo. Play a little audio to check the connection and adjust the volume before you set off.

Using a hired motorhome or rental car? Ask the hire team to help you pair your phone. You can also listen through your phone’s own speaker.

How do the driving stories start?

Your phone’s GPS tells the app when you reach a narration point along the tour route, and the story plays automatically. The Active Tour map shows your progress, the next story and what’s playing. Download your tour before leaving so the narration is ready for offline use.

Will the stories still play without mobile signal?

Yes. Your phone uses GPS satellites to work out where you are, so mobile reception or Wi-Fi isn’t needed to trigger the downloaded narration.

Install the app and fully download your chosen tour while you have a reliable connection, before you head out. Keep location access enabled, and you’re ready to hear the stories along the route even when mobile signal drops away.

Can I explore the tour before I travel?

Absolutely. Browse the route, places to visit, Bonus Stories, campsites and hotels before you leave. Pick out the stops that catch your eye and start shaping your trip.

Do I have to stop at every place?

Make the journey your own. Browse Things to See & Do, choose the places that interest you and take your time when you stop.

What are Bonus Stories?

At selected places, these longer stories give you more to discover when you stop. They’re a chance to spend a little longer with the history and characters of a place.

Can I find somewhere to stay?

The tour includes campsites and hotels along the route. Browse their details and facilities in the app before choosing where to settle in.
